What this page is

These are the train prompts the 4B policy answers with one stable, executor-accepted command in six or more of eight samples, and the gold disagrees. Each card shows the instruction, the buffer before, what the gold command does to it, and what the policy's answer does to it. Lines the command changed are marked; the cursor is shown as a highlighted cell; a trailing space is shown as · and a tab as ⇥.

Claude read every row and left a verdict with its reason. Yours is the one that counts. keep: the gold is right, teach it. retarget: the policy's reading is right, or the command below is; it becomes the gold and the expected buffer is recomputed. reword: the instruction is wrong or ambiguous; the text below becomes the instruction. drop: the row should not exist. unsure: come back later. Fields are prefilled with Claude's proposal and can be edited.

Keys: j / k move, 1–5 set keep / retarget / reword / drop / unsure on the focused card, a agrees with Claude. Verdicts save as you go; the export at the bottom is the change-set for apply_curation.py. Nothing is applied by this page.
